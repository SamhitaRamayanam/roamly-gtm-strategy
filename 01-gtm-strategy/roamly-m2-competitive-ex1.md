# Roamly Groups · Competitive Battlecard & Strategic Bet, Module 2 (Exercise 1)

## Responses
- **Competitor’s name:** Airbnb Experiences
- **Their core offering:** • Massive Catalog & Mindshare: Default search destination for leisure travelers in all major cities.
• Integrated Lodging Loop: Surfaces activities immediately after the organizer books an Airbnb home.
- **How they position themselves:** Unforgettable activities hosted by locals." Positioned around global brand ubiquity and seamless app-based booking attached to lodging.
- **Their primary audience:** Broad consumer travelers; solo tourists, couples, and casual groups looking for supplementary activities during a trip.
- **Known pricing or packaging:** Roamly operates on a transaction-based marketplace model consistent with premium experiential travel platforms. When packaging group bookings, split payments introduce multi-card interchange overhead
- **Why they’re your biggest threat:** Upstream Booking Monopoly: Group trips begin with lodging, not activities. Airbnb captures party size, dates, and intent at checkout, cross-selling its own experiences at zero customer acquisition cost (CAC).

Vulnerable Functional Wedge: Roamly’s core features—split payments and 24-hour calendar holds—are low-defensibility software mechanisms. Airbnb has the global payment and multi-guest infrastructure to replicate this workflow directly tied to existing home reservations.

Asymmetric Business Model: For Roamly, experience margins fund company growth. For Airbnb, experiences are a secondary retention layer for its lodging business, allowing them to undercut fees or subsidize group discounts as a loss leader.

Default Trust & Reach: Every guest in a group likely already has an active Airbnb account and saved payment methods. Roamly must overcome the friction of getting multiple unprompted users to trust an unfamiliar platform at checkout.

Supply Cannibalization: Because local experts cross-list, Airbnb introducing a native group hold could redirect top-tier host inventory away from Roamly toward Airbnb's higher-volume traveler base.
