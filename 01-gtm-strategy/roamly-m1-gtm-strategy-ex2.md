# Roamly Groups · GTM Strategy, Module 1 (Exercise 2)

## Responses
- **Which motion did you choose, PLG, SLG, CLG, or a hybrid? Explain why; if hybrid, what are your primary vs. secondary layers?:** The primary motion for Roamly Groups is Product-Led Growth (PLG).While Community-Led Growth (CLG) plays a supporting top-of-funnel role due to Roamly's trusted user base, the core mechanics of Roamly Groups align directly with a product-driven engine.
Organic Viral Loop: Every group created inherently invites multiple end users into the platform to view itineraries, vote, or split costs. Requiring a human sales touchpoint would stall this immediate collaborative utility.

Low-Friction Monetization: Because the buyer and end-user are identical (the trip leader) and contract values sit at consumer/SMB tiers, a self-serve funnel yields the lowest customer acquisition cost (CAC).

CLG as an Engine for PLG: While the core onboarding and activation engine must be self-serve (PLG), community engagement—sharing group templates, crowdsourcing local guides, and peer recommendations—serves as the top-of-funnel acquisition layer.
