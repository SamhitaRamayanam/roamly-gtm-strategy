Slide 02 / Strategy

GTM Strategy: Product-Led Checkout Rescue

Converting high-intent group drop-offs into an organic acquisition loop.

Motion & Strategic Rationale

  • Motion: Product-led checkout interception triggered at carts with 4+ guests.
  • Beachhead Segment: Reluctant Milestone Organizers coordinating 6–10 adult travelers.
  • Strategic Shift: Streamlining coordination into an automated utility without financial risk.

Slide 03 / Competitive Advantage

Competitive Intelligence & Strategic Bet

Exploiting incumbent distribution blind spots and execution friction.

Battlecard: Airbnb Experiences Vulnerability

  • Incumbent Edge: Controls lodging chronology; captures travel discovery at zero CAC.
  • Incumbent Flaw: Walled garden forces single-card checkout and mandatory app signups for all guests.
  • Coordination Friction: Planners must manually debt-collect via Venmo or risk open slots selling out.
  • Inventory Mismatch: Saturated with solo/low-cost tours rather than un-fuck-upable private buyouts.

The Strategic Bet

"We bet Roamly Groups wins by becoming the default coordination engine for high-stakes centerpiece celebrations—partnering upstream with vacation rental platforms to intercept planners post-lodging, removing personal credit liability, and turning social enforcement into our primary conversion engine."
  • Sacrifice 1: Collaborative consensus tools (no endless chat/debates).
  • Sacrifice 2: Low-cost, generic catalog tours ($30 solo walks).
Slide 04 / Positioning

Positioning Framework & Architecture

Reframing group coordination from administrative overhead to automated utility.

The Finalized Positioning Statement

For lead planners coordinating milestone celebrations, Roamly Groups is a frictionless group reservation utility that holds private calendar dates while collecting individual payments. Unlike traditional platforms that force one organizer to front the bill or mandate app downloads, Roamly eliminates personal liability through an open, 15-second guest payment link.

The From/To Market Shift

  • FROM: Trip organizers act as unpaid debt collectors, bearing 100% financial liability and tracking manual transfers.
  • TO: Coordination is an automated utility; organizers lock dates, participants self-settle, and the platform enforces collection.

The One-Liner Marketing Mantra

"Secure your event. Empower your crew. Let Roamly handle the rest."

Replaces administrative anxiety with structured relief and operational confidence.

Slide 07 / Insights

Strategic Insights & Execution Takeaways

Overcoming friction points and pivoting toward sustainable market differentiation.

Key Friction Points

  • The "Consensus Trap": Realizing collaborative voting tools slow checkout; compliance drives conversion.
  • Host Calendar Anxiety: Overcoming host fear of frozen dates with strict 24h drops and auto-cover backstops.
  • Walled-Garden Temptation: Resisting forced app signups to maintain a 15-second guest pay flow.

The Biggest "Aha" Moment

"Airbnb doesn't win on superior curation; they win on chronology."
  • Lodging dictates when activities are planned.
  • Competing on generic search is a losing CAC battle.
  • We must intercept organizers at lodging confirmation.

Execution Takeaways

  • Clear Sacrifices Define Strategy: Choosing what not to build protects velocity.
  • Incentive Alignment: De-risking both the organizer (liability) and host (liquidity) creates conversion.
  • Distribution Wedge: Upstream B2B2C property manager integrations beat direct search ads.
