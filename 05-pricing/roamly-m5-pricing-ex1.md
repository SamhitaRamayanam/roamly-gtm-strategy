# Roamly Groups · Pricing & Packaging Recommendation, Module 5 (Exercise 1)

## Responses
- **Pricing goal (Where to win first):** Beating Airbnb where they drop the ball: winning group travel by fixing split checkout, while driving high-margin revenue from large private bookings.
- **Why (Justification):** Our core bet established that Airbnb dominates travel chronology by capturing the lodging transaction, but fails at the checkout stage because forced single-card payments and mandatory guest accounts cause friction for group leaders. Our goal is to win the group’s single centerpiece event.



Introducing upfront SaaS fees, monthly subscriptions, or organizer-side platform charges would introduce top-of-funnel friction, driving organizers back to standard platforms and manual Venmo collection. The commercial objective in this launch phase must be rapid market penetration and liquidity generation: remove all barriers to booking initiation, enforce conversion through the 24-hour hold, and monetize the resulting transaction.
- **Pricing model (The model):** A Hybrid Transaction-Based Take-Rate with an Embedded Per-Seat Convenience Fee.



Value in Roamly Groups is discrete and episodic—it is experienced per booking event, not continuously. The customer derives value when the reservation is successfully locked, split, and paid without financial default.



• Supply-Side Take-Rate: 15% standard host commission on the total private buyout ticket value.


• Demand-Side Split Fee: A built-in 3% to 5% coordination/processing fee distributed proportionally across each guest's individual checkout link (e.g., $150 ticket + $6 processing = $156 total per attendee).
- **Who pays (Buyer vs. user):** The Organizer (The Buyer) pays zero platform fees upfront to create the hold. They receive peace of mind, automated coordination, and zero personal credit float for free.



The Participants (The Users/Payers) cover their exact mathematical share plus the fractional transaction fee. Because each guest is only paying an incremental $5 to $8 fee on a $150 to $250 luxury activity ticket via a 15-second Apple Pay flow, price sensitivity is negligible. The host absorbs the marketplace commission from the guaranteed group buyout, which they accept because Roamly eliminates empty slots and cancellations.
- **Packaging (The structure):** Packaging is structured By Products & Features, separating solo/pair public bookings from coordinated group buyouts.

From M4, we have three distinct stakeholders:

The Organizer needs risk mitigation, curation, and enforcement.

The Participant needs instant mobile payment, price transparency, and zero account creation.

The Host needs calendar liquidity and guaranteed revenue.

Because Roamly Groups is an end-to-end checkout orchestration engine, packaging must not gate basic utility (like link sharing or mobile pay). Instead, it packages features into clear service tiers that route group leaders away from standard individual booking into high-value private inventory.
- **Tiers (What each is for):** - Tier 1: Standard Individual / Public Bookings
Designed for: Solo travelers, couples, and casual daytime tours ($35–$60 AOV).

Inclusions: Standard single-card checkout, open public calendar slots, instant booking.

Fee Structure: Standard 15% host take-rate; 0% traveler fee.

Tier 2: Roamly Groups Private Buyout ( The Core Target Tier Routing 80%+ of Group Volume)

Designed for: Milestone Organizers coordinating 6 to 12 guests for private anchor experiences (catamarans, vineyard buyouts, chef tables with $1,000–$3,500 total booking value).

Inclusions:
24-Hour Calendar Hold Lock (secured via organizer pre-authorization with zero immediate charge).

No-login guest checkout web links (Apple Pay / Google Pay enabled).

Automated countdown clock and dynamic SMS/WhatsApp payment ledger ("4 of 8 paid").

Community-upvoted itinerary badge and curated host guarantee.

Fee Structure: 15% host commission + 4% participant-side split/processing fee.

Tier 3: Concierge Buyouts (High-Touch / Partner-Sourced)

Designed for: Luxury villa rentals, corporate retreats, and high-net-worth milestone groups ($4,000+ booking value) intercepted upstream via property management software (AvantStay, boutique villa concierges).

Inclusions: Custom private holds up to 48 hours, multi-vendor bundling (e.g., private chef + private yacht), offline invoice backup, and dedicated Roamly concierge coordination.

Fee Structure: 18% host take-rate + flat $99 organizer booking/concierge fee.
- **Which research method (Van Westendorp, Max-Diff, Conjoint) would you commission, and what specific assumption are you trying to validate?:** Conjoint Analysis (Discrete Choice Modeling)
- **What is the one thing in your recommendation that would change everything if you got it wrong?:** Validate the demand-side trade-off between participant convenience fees and guest checkout completion velocity.

Specifically, we need to test whether passing a 3% to 5% platform split fee to participants at the mobile-web checkout drawer causes cart abandonment compared to charging a flat fee to the organizer or fully baking the fee into an inflated upfront host ticket price.

The assumption that participants view a 3–5% split fee as an acceptable convenience charge rather than an unfair surcharge.



If participants balk at a visible coordination fee during mobile guest checkout and abandon their carts, the 24-hour hold will expire. Hosts will suffer high slot-release rates, destroying marketplace supply liquidity. If this assumption proves false, we must pivot to an all-inclusive host-absorbed pricing model where the take-rate is baked entirely into the advertised retail price of the private experience.
